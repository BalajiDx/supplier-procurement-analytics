# Supplier & Procurement Analytics

Supplier spend, delivery performance (OTIF), purchase price variance and supplier **change requests** (cost changes,
equipment price changes, units-per-tray updates) analysed with **SQL** and laid out as a **Power BI** report.

> **Synthetic data.** Suppliers, products, purchase orders and change requests were generated by
> `01. Dataset/generate_dataset.py` (seed 21). Supplier names are invented. No real company data is used.
> The work is modelled on the kind of supplier-change and procurement reporting I have built in Power BI, rebuilt
> here on made-up data so it can be shared.

## Business questions
1. Where does the money go: which suppliers and categories, and how is spend trending?
2. Which suppliers deliver late or short, and which categories are worst?
3. How far above contract are we paying (purchase price variance)?
4. Are supplier change requests decided within the 24-hour target, and does a bigger cost increase slow things down?
5. Which suppliers should procurement review?

## Key findings
| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total received spend was **₹50.06 Cr** across 8,849 POs; 2024 was **+0.8%** on 2023 (POs still open at 31 Dec 2024 are not counted). Cleaning (**+14.0%**) grew fastest; Dairy fell **16.5%**. | `01`, `02` |
| 2 | Spend is spread out: **16 of 28 suppliers** make up 80% of spend (class A). Packaging suppliers are class C but place many small POs. | `11` |
| 3 | Overall **OTIF is 82.0%** (on time 86.2%, in full 94.9%). Vaigai Pulses Pvt Ltd is lowest at **70.4%**; Beverages has the most late deliveries (**22.6%**). | `04`, `05` |
| 4 | PO prices ran **~4.7%** above contract cost in 2023 and **~10.6%** in 2024: **₹2.4 Cr** paid above contract in 2024. Contract costs were not updated as prices moved. | `06` |
| 5 | **91.3%** of 1,500 change requests were decided within 24 hours (130 missed). Requests for a **10%+** cost increase were rejected **25.0%** of the time and took **22.1 h** on average, against 4.9% and 11.9 h for 3-6%. | `07`, `08`, `09` |
| 6 | **7 suppliers** are flagged for review (OTIF below 75% or average requested increase above 6.5%), including the **#1 supplier by spend**, Chola Hygiene Pvt Ltd. | `12` |

**Recommendations (for the synthetic case):** refresh contract costs from approved change requests so PPV reflects real
overspend; put 10%+ increase requests on a fast track to the Category Manager; agree OTIF improvement plans with the
7 review suppliers, starting with the highest-spend ones.

## Data cleaning
| Table | Issue / action | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Suppliers | Supplier names trimmed and set to title case | 5 |
| Products | No issues found | 0 |
| PurchaseOrders | Duplicate POID rows removed | 45 |
| PurchaseOrders | UnitCost stored as text ('₹1,234.00') converted to number | 120 |
| PurchaseOrders | OrderDate in dd/mm/yyyy converted to yyyy-mm-dd | 60 |
| PurchaseOrders | Negative QtyReceived (sign error) made positive | 25 |
| PurchaseOrders | POs not yet received at 31 Dec 2024 flagged IsOpen = 1 | 151 |
| ChangeRequests | Duplicate RequestID rows removed | 30 |
| ChangeRequests | ChangeType text standardised | 39 |

## SQL scripts
| Script | Question |
|---|---|
| `01_Spend_By_Supplier.sql` | Which suppliers take the most spend (received value, 2023-2024), and what share of total? |
| `02_Spend_By_Category_YoY.sql` | How did spend change by category from 2023 to 2024? |
| `03_Monthly_Spend_Trend.sql` | What is the monthly spend trend, with a 3-month moving average? |
| `04_Supplier_OTIF_Scorecard.sql` | How does each supplier score on On-Time, In-Full (OTIF) and rejected quantity? |
| `05_Delivery_Delay_By_Category.sql` | Which categories deliver late most often, and by how many days? |
| `06_Purchase_Price_Variance.sql` | How far above the contract cost were POs priced, by category and year (purchase price variance)? |
| `07_Change_Requests_By_Type.sql` | How many supplier change requests came in by type, and how fast were they decided? |
| `08_Cost_Change_Bands.sql` | How big are the requested cost changes, and does the size affect approval and speed? |
| `09_Change_Request_SLA_Monthly.sql` | How many change requests missed the 24-hour decision target each month? |
| `10_Units_Per_Tray_Changes.sql` | Which approved units-per-tray updates change how many units a store receives per tray, and in which direction? |
| `11_Supplier_Pareto.sql` | How many suppliers make up 80% of spend (Pareto / ABC)? |
| `12_Vendor_Segmentation.sql` | Which suppliers need attention when spend, delivery (OTIF) and requested cost increases are combined? |
| `13_Open_POs_At_Cutoff.sql` | Which purchase orders were still not received at 31 Dec 2024, and how overdue are they? |

Techniques used: joins, CTEs, window functions (running totals, moving averages, `RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER`, share of total),
conditional aggregation, `CASE` banding and date arithmetic. Written for SQLite; notes for SQL Server / MySQL are in `00_Create_Tables_And_Load.sql`.
